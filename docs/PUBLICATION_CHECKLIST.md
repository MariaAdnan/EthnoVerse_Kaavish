# Cultural-record publication gate

This checklist is deliberately external to the upload form. It is an
operational gate, not evidence that consent already exists.

Before publishing each item, record and verify:

- stable archive item ID and file checksum;
- contributor, creator, custodian, and community authority where applicable;
- who granted permission and how their authority was established;
- permitted audience and uses, including research, education, public display,
  derivatives, and commercial use;
- required attribution and contextual description;
- culturally sensitive, sacred, location, gender, age, or seasonal access
  restrictions;
- whether names, voices, faces, or precise locations require redaction;
- permission date, evidence location, reviewer, and next review date;
- a contact and process for correction, restriction, or withdrawal; and
- the decision: public, restricted, embargoed, or do not publish.

If any required field is unknown, the safe state is **do not publish**. A
technical administrator must not infer community permission from possession of
a file. Any research study involving participants additionally requires the
institution's applicable ethics review and informed-consent process.
